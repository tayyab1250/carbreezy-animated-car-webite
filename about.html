Our story

Car buying should not feel like a negotiation

CarBreezy started with a simple frustration: the same car was priced five different ways in five different places on the same afternoon, and nobody would explain why.

We built a marketplace where every listing carries the same evidence — a 120-point inspection, a documented service history, a market benchmark and one clear price. Dealers and private sellers list side by side, judged on the same criteria.

  • One price, published in full, including taxes and delivery.
  • Ownership costs shown next to the sticker price.
  • Specialists on call instead of scripted sales desks.

How we work

Evidence over adjectives

Every claim on CarBreezy has to survive a data check. Mileage comes from service records and MOT history, not from a gauge photo. Condition comes from an inspection, not a description. Value comes from real transactions, not a model guess.

When the data is thin, we say so. A listing with a missing service record is labelled as one. That is why 98% of our customers would recommend us — because what we publish holds up.
